Brand name
LUKORY (루코리) is a coined name combining look and story: six Latin letters and three Korean syllables.
Its intended meaning is '얼굴 하나에서 시작되는 이야기' — a story beginning with a face.
The name expresses the community's shared first impressions and stories about faces.
